# Judge Q&A

## Why agentic AI?
Separate agents make data assumptions, optimization choices, and validation steps inspectable.

## Why not one prompt?
One prompt cannot reliably enforce budgets, dates, persistence, event trails, or reproducible benchmarks.

## What does CP-SAT optimize?
It selects feasible activities under budget, weather, diversity, preference, and distance-efficiency objectives.

## How is budget guaranteed?
The budget service reconciles transport, accommodation, activities, local transport, food, fees, contingency, total, and remaining.

## Which data is live?
Live weather is optional. Transport and accommodation prices are estimates unless explicitly labeled otherwise.

## How does fallback work?
Deterministic local providers keep the demo functional without paid APIs.

## How is it explainable?
Each plan includes source labels, assumptions, score explanations, validation warnings, and event history.

## What makes it research-worthy?
It combines multi-agent orchestration with constraint optimization and reproducible benchmark evaluation.

## What happens when providers fail?
Fallback data is used and labeled transparently.

## How does it remain free?
It uses open-source software, deterministic fallback providers, Render free services, and Neon free PostgreSQL.
