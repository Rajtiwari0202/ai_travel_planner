# Agent Catalog

- Intent agent: validates dates, travelers, budget, and preference shape.
- Destination research agent: loads destination overview and candidate activities.
- Transport research agent: prepares transparent estimated transport options.
- Accommodation research agent: estimates lodging with room-count assumptions.
- Weather agent: aligns weather guidance to trip dates.
- Geospatial agent: supports distance and route-efficiency scoring.
- Optimization agent: builds a budget-feasible schedule.
- Budget agent: reconciles cost categories.
- Writer agent: generates a grounded summary from structured facts.
- Critic validation agent: flags feasibility and data issues.
- Revision agent: replans from follow-up instructions.
